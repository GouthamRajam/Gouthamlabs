# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Our Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Providing helpful feedback on contributions
- Focusing on what is best for the project

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Any conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly.
